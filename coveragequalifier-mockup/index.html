Common questions

Clear answers before you choose.

Understand the basics and get help with the details that are specific to you.

What is term life insurance?

Term life insurance provides coverage for a selected period. If the insured person dies during that term and the policy is in force, the policy may pay a benefit to the named beneficiaries, subject to the policy terms.

Will I need a medical exam?

A medical exam is not required in most cases. Requirements depend on your application and underwriting review.

Why can my rate differ from the sample?

Sample rates are examples. Your actual premium depends on factors such as age, health, and other underwriting details.
