Consent you can review

TCPA-compliant data, backed by a certificate for every lead.

We design our lead collection around TCPA consent requirements and document the opt-in when a prospect submits their information. Every lead comes with a certificate recording the prospect’s submitted information, the time it was entered, the consent provided, and the prospect’s IP address.

Use the certificate to review what the prospect submitted and the scope of their consent before you reach out.

✓Lead submission certificateDocumentation supplied with the leadRECORD
Prospect informationDetails submitted with the inquiry
Submission timeDate and time recorded at submission
ConsentConsent record and applicable language
IP addressCaptured with the submission

Review the certificate attached to each lead to see its submission and consent record.

A certificate documents the opt-in event; it does not guarantee that every later call or message is lawful. Agents must follow the recorded consent, applicable calling rules, and their own compliance procedures.
