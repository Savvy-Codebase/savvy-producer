Savvy Leads · Live transfer

From first ring to your phone

You join once the lead has been reached, qualified and shown they want to talk to an agent.

  1. Step 1

    Outbound call

    A call goes out to the lead.

    StatusRinging the lead…
  2. No answer
    Step 2

    Contact

    The lead answers the phone. If nobody answers, a voicemail is left so they can call back.

    CallerHi, is this Maria? You asked about life insurance coverage online.
    StatusNo answer. Voicemail begins.
  3. No answer

    Voicemail left

    A voicemail is left so the lead can call back.

    VoicemailHi Maria, following up on your coverage request. Call us back at [callback number] any time today.
  4. Rejoins
    Callback

    Lead calls back

    The lead calls back and has the qualifying conversation, then rejoins at Step 3.

    CallerThanks for calling back, Maria. I just have a few quick questions.
    LeadSure, go ahead.
  5. Step 3

    Qualifying conversation

    A short conversation checks age range, state and coverage need.

    CallerWhat state are you in, and roughly what age range?
    LeadKentucky, mid fifties.
  6. Step 4

    Buyer intent measurement

    The call measures whether the lead wants to speak with an agent now.

    CallerWould you like to speak with a licensed agent right now?
    LeadYes, please.
  7. Is an agent available?
  8. Yes

    Transfer to agent

    The lead is connected to you live, already qualified.

    CallerGreat, I am connecting you to a licensed agent now.
  9. No

    Booked to your calendar

    A callback is set for the same day, so the lead is not lost.

    CallerNo agent is free right now. What time today works for a callback?
    LeadAround four.
