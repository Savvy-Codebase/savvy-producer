Jason understands the work of growing an insurance business because he has built a successful career in the industry. Over more than 26 years in sales, he has gained experience in insurance, mortgage brokerage, sales leadership, and marketing. As a life insurance producer, Jason earned national recognition as a top performer and generated multi-millions in annualized premiums over 15 years as an active producer with cutting-edge technology he developed.

His experience has also made him a sought-after guest speaker at industry conferences, where he shares lessons from his success so other professionals can learn and grow. Jason has worked with hundreds of agents, who value his keen understanding of the business and practical guidance.

His favorite part of the work is helping agents build their book of business and reach new heights in their careers.
