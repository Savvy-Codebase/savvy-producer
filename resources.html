Before you get started

Answers to common questions about SavvyLeads products, SavvyMarketing campaigns, and working with Savvy Producer. Availability, product terms, and campaign details can vary; confirm the specifics with our team before ordering.

Lead types and quality

Options include Final Expense, Medicare, SSDI, and General Life, depending on current availability. Formats may include live transfers, booked appointments, real-time lead flow, aged data, and Final Expense leads or reserved territory through the SavvyLeads map. Ask us what is currently available for your product and state.

SavvyMarketing says its leads come from its own media and vetted publishers, with TCPA consent captured at opt-in and DNC and duplicate screening before delivery. Ask which source and consent records apply to the specific product you are considering.

SavvyLeads lists a name, full street address, city, state, and phone number, with date of birth on most records. Its real-time records also include TrustedForm, Jornaya, and the IP captured at the form. Included fields vary by product, so confirm the record details before ordering.

Real-time leads are delivered close to when an inquiry is submitted. Aged leads are older inquiries. Timing, included fields, and the best fit depend on the product and your follow-up process.

SavvyLeads says to send the record and the reason for review; wrong numbers, bad addresses, or records that do not match what was promised may be credited to the account. SavvyMarketing describes credits or replacements for wrong numbers, disconnects, and clear data errors reported within its return window. Ask for the current return window and terms for your specific order.

Territory and delivery

It depends on the product. SavvyMarketing says live transfers, booked appointments, and real-time orders go to one agent; reserved territory can be held for an agent, while shared or aged leads are identified before purchase. SavvyLeads says each record on its map is sold once, while a territory reservation gives first claim on new leads within that area. Confirm the distribution terms for the product you select.

SavvyLeads says you can select counties and ZIP codes or draw a radius around your office. Territory choices and inventory depend on the product and current availability.

A reservation gives you first claim on new leads within a defined area, subject to its terms and cap. Overflow is inventory already available on the map that was not claimed by a reserved area. Ask us to confirm the current pricing and details.

SavvyLeads says overflow orders are available after checkout and real-time leads arrive when the form is submitted. SavvyMarketing says real-time leads post within seconds, either to your CRM or dialer or as a file. Delivery timing and integrations vary by product.

SavvyLeads says records can be downloaded as a CSV and real-time leads may be posted to your system. SavvyMarketing also describes CRM or dialer delivery for real-time leads. Confirm supported integrations and setup requirements with the team.

SavvyMarketing says most states are available, though volume changes over time. Share your licensed states and product focus so the team can confirm current availability.

Campaigns, pricing, and getting started

A prospect is contacted, has a brief conversation, and may be connected to an available agent. Qualification criteria, routing, availability, and billing rules depend on the transfer tier; confirm them before launch.

Pricing depends on product, state, and volume. SavvyMarketing says many agents begin with a small test order rather than a commitment. SavvyLeads says overflow orders have no minimum or contract, while territory reservations have a weekly cap you set and can be paused when full. Ask for the current terms for your choice.

Terms vary by product. SavvyLeads states that overflow orders have no minimum or contract; reserved areas use a weekly cap that can be paused. Confirm the commitment, cap, and cancellation terms for any product before ordering.

Results depend on factors such as follow-up speed, call flow, product, and agent execution. SavvyMarketing says it will discuss what agents with similar volume see but does not promise a close rate. No lead or transfer can guarantee a sale.

Contact the team with the products you offer, your licensed states, and the support you need. They can review current availability and outline what a first order or campaign could look like.
