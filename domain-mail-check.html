svmktg.net and svprod.org. Records read from the IONOS zones on 23 October 2026.

Why a perfect setup matters. Receiving servers decide whether to trust a message by checking the sending domain. SPF proves the mail came from an approved server, DKIM proves it was not altered and was signed by this domain, and DMARC ties both to the visible From address and tells receivers what to do with mail that fails. With all three passing and aligned, as the test messages show, Gmail and Yahoo can verify the sender, no one can spoof these domains without being quarantined, and the daily DMARC reports show any problem before it costs placement. It is the foundation that warmup, clean lists and good copy build on. It does not guarantee the inbox on its own.

What lifts both scores
  • Re-test under the quarantine policy (no points). Both test messages were sent before the switch to p=quarantine and passed under p=none. Send one more from each domain and confirm dmarc=pass with p=QUARANTINE.
  • Authorize cross-domain DMARC reports (no points, needed for delivery). Reports go to savvyproducer.com, a different domain. Add a TXT record svmktg.net._report._dmarc.savvyproducer.com and svprod.org._report._dmarc.savvyproducer.com, each with the value v=DMARC1, in the savvyproducer.com DNS (Cloudflare). Without them many receivers skip sending reports.
  • Watch the DMARC reports before sending volume (no points). Policy is p=quarantine now, so any message that fails SPF or DKIM alignment goes to spam. Confirm dkim=pass on a real message first.
