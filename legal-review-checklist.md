# Legal pages — publication checklist

The current drafts are `privacy.html` and `terms.html`. Both pages are linked from the homepage footer. They are intentionally labeled as drafts until these items are confirmed.

## Confirm before publication

- [ ] Exact legal operator for Savvy Producer, Savvy Leads, and each service; confirm whether SavvyMarketing LLC is the operator or only an affiliated brand.
- [ ] Exact mailing address. Existing notes conflict on whether the suite is `#2202`.
- [ ] A monitored privacy contact email and the method for receiving, verifying, tracking, and responding to data-rights requests and appeals.
- [ ] Every active website form, lead form, campaign/publisher source, consent language, collection field, and recipient of prospect data. Make sure point-of-collection disclosures match actual lead routing, calls, texts, and email.
- [ ] Whether any personal information is sold or shared for targeted advertising, and which state-law opt-out controls are required. Confirm whether each operating entity meets applicable privacy-law thresholds.
- [ ] Current vendors and data flows, including hosting, CRM, email/SMS, call recording, consent verification, chat, analytics, advertising pixels, and Google Fonts. Add only vendors actually used.
- [ ] Retention periods for inquiries, lead records, consent evidence, account data, and suppression records; confirm the actual deletion and opt-out process.
- [ ] Product-specific contract terms for pricing, payment, credits/returns, cancellations, warranties, indemnity, and dispute resolution. Keep those in the order or campaign agreement where appropriate.
- [ ] Have U.S. privacy/advertising counsel review the final notices against actual practices and all states where leads are collected or marketed.

Kentucky's Consumer Data Protection Act took effect January 1, 2026. Applicability depends on the entity and statutory thresholds; if covered, its privacy notice and consumer-request rules need to be reflected in the final policy. See the [Kentucky statute](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/Law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=55839) and [consumer-rights provision](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/Statutes/statute.aspx?id=55838).

These website terms do not replace the CAN-SPAM requirements and opt-out details for commercial outreach emails.
