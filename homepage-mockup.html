Before you get started

Here are a few of the details agents commonly want to discuss.

Real-time leads are delivered close to the time an inquiry is submitted. Aged leads are older inquiries. The best fit depends on your follow-up process and the availability of each product.

We’ll confirm the specific exclusivity terms for the product and territory with you before you order. Ask our team how exclusivity is defined and handled for your selected lead flow.

A prospect is contacted, has a brief conversation, and may be connected to an available agent. Qualification criteria, routing, availability, and billing rules should be confirmed with our team for each transfer tier.

Territory and delivery options depend on product availability. Contact us with your licensed states and preferred volume, and we’ll review what is currently available.
