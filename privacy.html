1. Scope

This policy applies to information handled through the Savvy Producer and Savvy Leads websites, our communications with prospective business customers, and lead-generation, appointment, and live-transfer programs we operate or arrange. A separate notice or agreement may apply to a particular campaign, product, or service. If another notice conflicts with this one for that service, the more specific notice controls to the extent permitted by law.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us and which service is involved, information may include:

  • Business contact information: name, business name, email address, phone number, role, and mailing address.
  • Lead and inquiry information: name, phone number, email, mailing address, state or ZIP code, date of birth where relevant to a product, insurance interests, requested products, and contact preferences.
  • Campaign and consent records: the form or source that generated an inquiry, date and time, IP address, URL or page, consent language shown, and related verification records supplied by a publisher or consent-record provider.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser and device details, pages requested, referring page, and basic server logs. Our pages currently request fonts from Google Fonts, which means a visitor’s browser connects to Google to retrieve those fonts.
  • Communications: the content of messages, questions, support requests, and information you choose to provide.

Please do not send us Social Security numbers, payment-card details, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through a general inquiry channel.

3. How information is collected

We may collect information directly when you contact us or request information; automatically through website requests and server logs; and from advertising publishers, campaign partners, referral sources, service providers, or other parties involved in a lead program. The source, form, and consent language for a particular lead can vary. We will provide or link the campaign-specific notice at the point of collection where required.

4. How we use information

We may use information to respond to inquiries; evaluate and deliver requested products or services; create, verify, route, and support leads, appointments, or live transfers; communicate about campaigns and account matters; maintain consent and suppression records; operate, secure, and improve our services; prevent fraud or misuse; meet legal obligations; and establish or defend legal claims.

5. How information may be shared

We may disclose information to service providers who help host, secure, communicate, manage, or support our services; to affiliates and companies operating under the Savvy brands where needed to provide a requested service; and to insurance agents, agencies, call centers, or campaign buyers who receive a lead, appointment, or transfer. For a lead-generation program, this may be a compensated transfer of the prospect’s information to the agent or agency selected or identified for that program. The campaign notice and consent shown when the information is collected should describe the relevant recipients and contact methods.

We may also disclose information to professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or complete a corporate transaction. We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for unrelated purposes.

6. Cookies, analytics, and third-party services

Our current pages use Google Fonts. The browser requests font files from Google; Google may receive technical request information under its own privacy practices. We do not currently see an analytics or advertising-pixel script in the local homepage code. If analytics, advertising, chat, embedded media, or other tracking technologies are added or enabled by the hosting platform, this section must be updated to name the tools, explain their purposes, and describe any choices or consent controls that apply.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including providing services, maintaining business and consent records, honoring opt-out requests, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and service. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain processing such as targeted advertising, sale, or profiling. You may also have the right to appeal a decision on a privacy request. To submit a request, email hello@savvyleads.io with “Privacy Request” in the subject line and describe your request and the relevant interaction. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may designate an authorized agent where the law allows.

For marketing email, use the unsubscribe method in the message or contact us at the address below. We may retain limited information on a suppression list so we can honor your choice. Opting out of marketing messages does not stop service or account communications that are not marketing.

9. Children

Our websites and services are intended for business users and adults, not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, vendors, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
